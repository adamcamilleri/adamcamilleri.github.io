Welcome to the Self-Regulation Intervention Simulator

This interactive tool allows you to experience evidence-based self-regulation interventions including paced breathing, mindfulness, expressive writing, gratitude journaling, nature visualization, and more. Rate your mood before and after each exercise, and view how different interventions affect emotional states over time.

All data is stored locally on your device. No information is sent to any server.
