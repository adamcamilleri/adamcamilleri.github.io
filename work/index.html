  8. 09:00 nobody notices. that's the job.

What I actually do

I'm a data analyst on the Reporting & Analytics team at MCAP, one of Canada's largest independent mortgage financing companies. My work sits in the middle of the pipeline: source data lives in DB2 on an IBM AS/400 (a platform older than I am and more reliable than most things newer), and business units need it turned into reports they can act on.

The bridge is SSIS package development: extract jobs that pull from DB2, transformations that reshape and validate, and deployments that publish finished reports on a schedule. The SQL runs against both the AS/400 side and SQL Server, and the deliverables land where the business actually lives: mostly Excel.
